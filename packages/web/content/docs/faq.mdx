title: FAQ
description: Answers about NEAR Testnet token gifts.
---

## What can I gift?

Any NEP-141 token contract that the Santa contract owner has allowlisted. The browser only offers the configured token IDs. Test tokens have no monetary value.

## Who pays the fees?

The sender pays the NEAR Testnet gas for the initial token transfer. Santa's configured relayer pays testnet gas for claims. This is a testnet feature and does not guarantee sponsored transactions on mainnet.

## Does the recipient need a wallet?

Yes. The recipient connects a NEAR Testnet wallet and signs a NEP-413 message proving control of the destination account. Santa does not create an embedded wallet for them.

## What if I lose the gift link?

The claim secret is in the link fragment and is not stored by the contract or server. Keep a private copy. If the gift expires unclaimed, the original sender can request a refund.

## Is a refund automatic?

No. The sender must call the refund method after expiry. Refunds depend on the NEP-141 contract accepting the transfer.

## Is the app on mainnet?

No. The active implementation is configured for NEAR Testnet and is not audited for production use.
