---
title: Santa
description: Private, sponsored NEAR Testnet token gifts.
---

Santa is a NEAR Testnet gifting app for allowlisted NEP-141 tokens. Send a token gift with a note encrypted in your browser, share a private link, and let the recipient claim with their NEAR wallet. Santa sponsors claim gas through a dedicated relayer.

The active app is testnet-only. Test tokens have no monetary value.

Start with [getting started](/docs/getting-started), read [how gifts work](/docs/how-it-works), or inspect the [NEAR contract](/docs/contracts).
