# Santa brand assets

PNG logos live in `packages/web/public` and are served from the site root.

| Asset | Size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [logo.png](../packages/web/public/logo.png) | 1200 × 400 | Burgundy Santa wordmark on a transparent background; use on light surfaces. |
| [logo-light.png](../packages/web/public/logo-light.png) | 1200 × 400 | Ivory Santa wordmark on a transparent background; use on dark surfaces. |
| [logo-square.png](../packages/web/public/logo-square.png) | 1024 × 1024 | Gold envelope mark on a solid burgundy background; use for avatars and square placements. |
| [icon.svg](../packages/web/app/icon.svg) | Vector | Current app icon. |

The wordmarks follow Santa’s lowercase Fraunces lettering and eight-spoke star. Their outlined vector masters are [logo.svg](../packages/web/public/logo.svg) and [logo-light.svg](../packages/web/public/logo-light.svg), so displaying them requires no external fonts.

Keep the original proportions and surrounding clear space. The primary burgundy is `#5B1E2E`; the light wordmark is `#FFF8E8`. Wordmark outlines use the bundled Fraunces Bold font; its [SIL Open Font License](../packages/web/app/og/Fraunces-OFL.txt) is included in the repository.
